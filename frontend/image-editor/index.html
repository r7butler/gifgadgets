How to Edit a Photo

  1. 1

    Upload your photo

    Click the upload button above or drag a photo onto it. Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC.

  2. 2

    Add text or title bars

    Drag caption overlays anywhere on the image, or add a top/bottom title bar with a custom background colour.

  3. 3

    Adjust and filter

    Use the Adjustments panel to tune brightness, contrast, saturation, apply filters, crop, or resize.

  4. 4

    Export and download

    Choose JPG, PNG, or WebP and click Download. Your edited photo is saved directly to your device.

Is this tool free?
Yes — completely free. No account, no watermark, no limits.
Does my photo get uploaded to a server?
No. All editing runs in your browser. Your photos never leave your device.
Can I add multiple captions?
Yes. You can add as many on-image text overlays as you like, each with its own font, size, colour, and position.
