Frequently asked questions

Does PNG to WebP preserve transparency?
Yes. WebP supports alpha transparency, so transparent areas in your PNG will remain transparent in the WebP output.
Is WebP lossless like PNG?
WebP supports both lossless and lossy modes. This converter uses lossy mode with your chosen quality setting. For pixel-perfect lossless output, keep using PNG.
How much smaller will the WebP be?
WebP is typically 50–80% smaller than PNG for photographic content and 20–40% smaller for graphics with transparency.
Should I use WebP instead of PNG on my website?
For most web use cases yes — WebP supports transparency, is smaller, and is supported by all modern browsers. Use PNG only when lossless quality is strictly required.
Is my file uploaded anywhere?
No. All conversion happens in your browser. Your PNG file never leaves your device.
