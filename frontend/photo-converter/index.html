Why convert photo formats?

Compatibility

Some apps, platforms, or devices only accept specific formats. Converting ensures your image works everywhere.

File Size

JPG and WebP are much smaller than PNG for photographs. Converting can cut file sizes by 50–80%.

Transparency

PNG and WebP support alpha transparency while JPG does not. Convert when your use case requires it.

Web Performance

WebP offers the best quality-to-size ratio for web images. Convert JPG/PNG to WebP to speed up your site.

Frequently asked questions

Are these photo converters really free?
Yes. Every converter on GifWidgets is free to use with no hidden fees, no watermarks, and no signup required.
Do my photos get uploaded to a server?
No. All conversions happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your photos never leave your device.
Which format should I choose for photos?
For photographs, JPG or WebP are ideal due to their small file sizes. Use PNG for screenshots or graphics that need sharp edges and lossless quality. Use WebP for the best web performance.
Is there a file size limit?
There is no enforced limit. Very large files (above ~50 MB) may be slow to process depending on your device's memory and browser, but most images convert in under a second.
What browsers are supported?
All modern browsers are supported: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. HEIC conversion uses a JavaScript library (heic2any) that works in all these browsers.
