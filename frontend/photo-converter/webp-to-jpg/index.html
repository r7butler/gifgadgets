Frequently asked questions

Why convert WebP to JPG?
WebP is not universally supported. Some email clients, printers, older operating systems and apps don't handle WebP. Converting to JPG ensures your image works everywhere.
Will I lose quality?
There may be minor quality loss since JPG is lossy. Setting quality to 90 or above produces results visually indistinguishable from the original for most images.
Does this work with animated WebP?
Only the first frame is extracted and converted to a static JPG. Animated WebP files are not fully converted frame-by-frame.
Is my file uploaded to a server?
No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device.
What browsers support WebP?
All modern browsers — Chrome, Firefox, Safari 14+, and Edge — support WebP. If you're using an older browser, consider updating it.
