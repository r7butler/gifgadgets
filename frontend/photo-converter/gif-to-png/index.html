    How to use GIF to PNG & Frame Extractor

    1. Choose an animated GIF from your device.
    2. Keep frame 1 for the first-frame converter, enter a range such as 2-8, or choose all frames. Export up to 500 frames at a time.
    3. Select Apply and preview, then download the result.

    Large dimensions or many frames may exceed the local memory limit even below 40 MB. Resize or shorten the source if needed.

    PNG frames include the complete composed image, including content retained from earlier frames. PNG exports preserve transparency.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which frame does this convert by default?
    Frame 1, which is the classic GIF to PNG behaviour. You can also enter a range such as 2-8 or choose All frames to export the whole animation.
    Is GIF transparency preserved in the PNG?
    Yes. PNG supports alpha transparency, so transparent areas in the GIF are preserved in the exported PNG.
    Can I extract every frame from an animated GIF?
    Yes. Choose All frames, up to 500 at a time. A single frame downloads as a PNG; several frames download as a ZIP, with each frame also listed individually below the tool.
    Do exported frames include content from earlier frames?
    Yes. Each PNG is the fully composed canvas at that point in the animation, not just the small region that changed, so every frame looks the way it does during playback.
    Why use PNG instead of JPG?
    PNG preserves GIF transparency, which JPG cannot, and it is lossless, so no quality is lost beyond the GIF's own fidelity.
    Is my GIF uploaded to a server?
    No. Frames are decoded and written to PNG entirely in your browser.
