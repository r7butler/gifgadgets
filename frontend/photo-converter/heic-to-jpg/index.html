Frequently asked questions

What is HEIC format?
HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format on iPhones running iOS 11 and later. It uses advanced compression to produce smaller files than JPG with similar quality, but is not universally supported.
Why convert HEIC to JPG?
JPG is supported by virtually every app, website, printer, and device. Converting ensures your iPhone photos work everywhere.
How do I get HEIC files off my iPhone?
Connect via USB and set "Transfer to Mac or PC" to "Keep Originals" in iPhone Settings > Photos. You can also AirDrop photos to a Mac, or email them to yourself.
Is my HEIC file uploaded to a server?
No. The heic2any library runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your photos never leave your device.
What quality should I use for iPhone photos?
Quality 90 is recommended — excellent JPG output very close to the original. For web sharing, 70–80 is fine and produces much smaller files.
