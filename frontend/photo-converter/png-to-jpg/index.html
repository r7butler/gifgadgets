Frequently asked questions

Why does my transparent PNG background become white in JPG?
JPG does not support transparency. This converter fills transparent areas with white before encoding. If you need to keep transparency, convert to WebP instead.
What quality setting should I use?
Quality 90 is a good default for most uses. For web thumbnails 70–80 is fine. For high-quality archival images use 95–100.
Will I lose quality?
Yes — JPG is lossy. Higher quality settings preserve more detail at the cost of larger files.
Is EXIF metadata preserved?
No. The Canvas API strips metadata during conversion. The output JPG will not contain EXIF or other embedded data.
Is my file uploaded anywhere?
No. All conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API.
