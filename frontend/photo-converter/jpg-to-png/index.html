Frequently asked questions

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?
No. The PNG output will be at least as good as your JPG, but it cannot recover quality lost during JPEG compression. For a true quality improvement you would need the original source image.
Why is the PNG larger than the JPG?
PNG stores full pixel data without lossy compression, so files are naturally larger than JPG equivalents. That's a trade-off for higher quality.
Will the converted PNG have a transparent background?
No. JPG files have no transparency channel, so the PNG output will have an opaque background matching the original image.
Is this safe to use for private or sensitive photos?
Yes. The entire conversion happens locally in your browser. No image data is ever sent to any server.
Can I convert multiple JPG files at once?
Currently each conversion is one file at a time. Select a file, convert it, download, then repeat for the next image.
