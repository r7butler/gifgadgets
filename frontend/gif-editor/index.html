How to Add Text to a GIF

  1. 1

    Upload your GIF or video

    Click the upload button above or drag a file directly onto it. Supports .gif, .mp4, .webm, .mov, and .avi files. Videos are automatically converted to GIF.

  2. 2

    Add captions

    Click + Add in the sidebar to create a caption. Type your text, then drag it to the right spot on the GIF.

  3. 3

    Customize and time it

    Use the sidebar to change font, size, and color. Drag the timeline handles to set the exact frames where the caption appears.

  4. 4

    Download or share

    Hit Download to save your captioned GIF. Or use Share to get a public link you can share anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add text to a GIF online?
Upload your GIF to GifWidgets, click Add Caption, type your text, and drag it anywhere. Set timing with the visual timeline, then download.
Is this GIF editor free?
Yes — completely free. No account, no subscription, no watermark on your exports.
Can I add multiple captions to one GIF?
Yes. Add as many captions as you like, each with its own timing, position, font, and color settings.
Are my files uploaded to a server?
Editing runs entirely in your browser — your files stay on your device. Sharing via a public link uploads only when you explicitly consent.
Does the GIF editor work on mobile?
Yes. It works in any modern browser on iOS and Android, including Chrome and Safari.
