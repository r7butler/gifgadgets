How to Add Animated Captions to a GIF

  1. 1

    Upload your GIF or video

    Click upload or drag a GIF, MP4, WebM, MOV, or AVI file. Videos are converted to GIF automatically.

  2. 2

    Add a caption and position it

    Click + Add to create a caption, type your text, and drag it to the starting position.

  3. 3

    Add motion keyframes or use AI tracking

    Seek to different frames and click + Keyframe here to pin the caption at that position. Or click Follow an Object and tap the target — the AI will do the rest.

  4. 4

    Set timing, crop, compress, and export

    Use the timeline to set caption start/end frames. Optionally crop or compress the output. Hit Download.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can this GIF editor do?
It includes motion keyframes, AI object tracking, zoom up to 8×, crop, GIF compression, and all the standard caption features for fast text overlays.
How does AI object tracking work?
Select a caption, click "Follow an Object", then click on the target in the preview. The SAM2 model creates motion keyframes that follow it across every frame.
Can I animate text captions?
Yes. Use "Keyframe here" at different frames with different positions. The caption will smoothly interpolate between them.
Is this free?
Yes — completely free. No account, no watermark.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The GIF editor works in modern mobile browsers, including Safari and Chrome on iPhone and Android.
