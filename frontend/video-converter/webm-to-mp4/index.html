About WebM to MP4 conversion

MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14) is the most universally supported video format. Converting WebM files to MP4 ensures compatibility with virtually all devices, operating systems, and media players — from iPhones and Android devices to Windows Media Player and smart TVs.

This converter uses libx264 for H.264 video encoding and AAC for audio — the industry standard combination for maximum compatibility. All processing happens locally in your browser; your file is never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert WebM to MP4?
WebM is not natively supported by many devices, including iPhones, iPads, most smart TVs, and Windows Media Player. Converting to MP4 gives you a file that plays everywhere.
Will quality be degraded?
There will be some re-encoding quality loss, but at the default medium preset the difference is typically imperceptible for most content. Choose the slow preset for best quality at the cost of longer conversion time.
Can I share the MP4 on social media?
Yes. MP4 with H.264 encoding is accepted by virtually all social platforms including Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, and YouTube.
Is my file private?
Yes. Conversion happens entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server.
What browsers are supported?
Chrome and Edge work reliably. Recent Firefox versions also work. Safari support depends on the version.
