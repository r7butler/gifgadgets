The Easiest Way to Add Text to GIFs

GifCaption is a free online tool that lets you add text captions and overlays to any animated GIF. Unlike other GIF editors, GifCaption gives you frame-level precision — you control exactly which frames each caption appears on using a visual timeline. Drag text anywhere on the image, customize fonts, colors, sizes, and alignment, and export a perfectly captioned GIF in seconds.

Everything runs entirely in your browser. Your GIFs are never uploaded to a server, so your content stays private. There's no account to create, no watermark on your exports, and no limit on how many GIFs you can edit.

How It Works

  1. 1

  2. 2

  4. 4

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add text to a GIF?
Upload your GIF to GifCaption, click "Add Caption", type your text, and drag it to position. Use the visual timeline to control exactly which frames the caption appears on, then export your captioned GIF.
Is GifCaption free?
Yes, GifCaption is completely free to use. There is no signup required, no watermark added to your GIFs, and no limit on how many GIFs you can caption.
Can I add multiple captions to a GIF?
Yes. You can add as many text captions as you want, each with independent timing, positioning, font, color, and size settings.
Does GifCaption work on mobile?
Yes, GifCaption works in any modern mobile browser. You can upload GIFs, add captions, and export directly from your phone or tablet.
What file formats does GifCaption support?
GifCaption supports animated GIF files as well as video files (MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI) which are converted to GIF for captioning.
Are my GIFs uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device unless you choose to share a captioned GIF via a link.
