Frequently Asked Questions

Is the video editor free?
Yes — completely free, no signup, no watermark.
What output format does it produce?
WebM, using the browser's native MediaRecorder API. WebM is supported in Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. For MP4 output, use the Video Converters to convert the WebM to MP4.
Does my video get uploaded to a server?
No. The video is processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
What video formats can I upload?
MP4, WebM, MOV (QuickTime), and AVI. Browser support for MOV and AVI depends on your browser's codecs.
