How to Make a GIF from Images

  1. 1

    Upload your images

    Click upload or drag multiple image files onto the button. Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and HEIC.

  2. 2

    Arrange and configure

    Drag frames to reorder them. Set the delay between frames and choose whether to resize images to match.

  3. 3

    Optional: enable crossfade

    Turn on crossfade for smooth transitions between frames. Adjust the number of intermediate frames.

  4. 4

    Download your GIF

    Click Make GIF and download the result. You can also open it in the GIF Editor to add captions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a GIF from images?
Upload your images, arrange them in order, set the delay, and click Make GIF. Download the result when ready.
What image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and HEIC. You can mix different formats in the same GIF.
What happens if my images are different sizes?
You can choose to resize all images to match the smallest one, or keep original sizes (smaller images will be centered with empty space around them).
What is crossfade?
Crossfade generates intermediate frames that smoothly blend one image into the next, creating a slideshow-like transition.
Can I add captions to the GIF?
Yes! Open the downloaded GIF in the GIF Editor to add text, captions, and more.
