Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize a GIF?
Upload your GIF, choose a new width, and click Resize. The height adjusts automatically.
Will resizing break the animation?
No. All frames are resized and the original timing is preserved.
Does resizing reduce file size?
Yes. Fewer pixels per frame means a significantly smaller file.
Can I add captions after resizing?
Yes! Open the resized GIF in the GIF Editor to add text and captions.
