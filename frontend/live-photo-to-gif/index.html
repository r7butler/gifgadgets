Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an iPhone Live Photo to GIF?
First export the .mov: in Photos, tap Share → Save Video. Then upload the .mov here and click Convert.
How do I export a Live Photo on Mac?
In the Photos app on Mac: select the Live Photo → File → Export → Export Unmodified Original. Upload the .mov that's included in the export.
Why does my Live Photo look short?
Live Photos are only about 3 seconds long by design — that's the full clip. To get a longer GIF, you may want to use a regular video instead.
