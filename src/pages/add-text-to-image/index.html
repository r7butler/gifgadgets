Add Text to Image

Add text to a photo, label a screenshot, or make a captioned meme. Style your words with custom fonts, colors, and outlines — free, with no signup.

Upload an image to add text

Write on your photos

Add multiple captions, titles, and labels. Drag each text overlay into position and choose its font, size, and color.

Make text stand out

Customize the outline color and width to keep words readable over a busy photo. Use separate captions for different text styles.

Add text above or below an image

Use top and bottom caption bars to keep text off the picture. Adjust background colors, borders, height, and text alignment.

Save your captioned image

Download as JPG or PNG, with WebP available in supported browsers. Adjust export quality and turn off the optional watermark in Other Options.

How to add text to a photo online

  1. 1

    Choose your image

    Upload or drag in a JPG, PNG, WebP, or HEIC photo. You can also use a screenshot or other still image.

  2. 2

    Type your caption

    Open On-Image Captions, click + Add, and enter your text. Add another caption for a separate title or label.

  3. 3

    Style and position your text

    Choose a font, size, fill color, and outline. Drag the caption onto your photo. To place text outside the picture, open Box Caption and add a top or bottom bar.

  4. 4

    Download the finished image

    Choose your export format in Other Options, then click Download. Your text is saved as part of the picture.

Make photo captions easy to read

Keep titles short and use a contrasting outline over detailed backgrounds. Leave space around faces and important objects. A top or bottom caption bar gives longer text room without covering the photo. Preview at the size you plan to share so your words remain readable.
